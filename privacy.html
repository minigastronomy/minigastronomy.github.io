Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Mini Gastronomy Uploader is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Mini Gastronomy YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only with the owner's own Google account.

What the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read basic information about that channel (such as its name and ID).

How data is used and stored

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's own systems and is used solely for the uploads described above. The app does not collect, sell, share or disclose data about any other users, and does not send Google user data to third parties.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
